  1. arXiv
    The Resolution Hypothesis: Agent Evolution’s Key Is Identifying Improvements
    Yuanhao Zeng*, Shaocheng Lan*, Ao Lu*, and 8 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2611.23068, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML PDF Blog Website

    Agent evolution follows a simple loop: a proposer writes candidate artifacts (prompts, skills, memories) and selection accepts those that score higher. Across seven evolution methods and six models (over 10M LLM calls), we trace why methods trade wins across papers and stall on coding tasks to the Resolution Hypothesis: agent evolution stalls not because methods fail to propose better artifacts, but because noisy evaluations cannot reliably identify them.

  2. ICML
    Large Language Models Explore by Latent Distilling
    Yuanhao Zeng, Ao Lu, Lufei Li, and 3 more authors
    In International Conference on Machine Learning, 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code

    Exploratory Sampling (ESamp) is a decoding-time method for large language models that encourages semantic diversity during generation. It trains a lightweight Latent Distiller online to predict deep-layer hidden representations from shallow-layer representations; the prediction error acts as a novelty signal for reweighting candidate continuations toward less-explored reasoning paths.
